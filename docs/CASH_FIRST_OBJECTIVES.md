# Cash-First Objectives

## North Star

Collect the first legal, verified, non-charcoal euro autonomously.

Louis OS must optimize for verified net cash, not activity volume. Research, messages, leads, drafts, and actions are intermediate signals only.

## Mandatory commercial loop

1. Detect a qualified need.
2. Generate a concrete paid offer.
3. Contact a buyer through an allowed channel.
4. Follow up until a bounded stopping rule is reached.
5. Negotiate scope, price, proof, and payment path.
6. Record payment evidence or the precise rejection reason.
7. Continue, pivot, stop, or accelerate based on measured outcomes.

## Required KPIs

- first payment received;
- gross and net revenue;
- margin;
- qualified response rate;
- conversion rate;
- follow-up count;
- time to first payment;
- revenue by strategy;
- rejection reasons;
- strategy abandonment rate.

## Required cycle questions

Every autonomous monetization cycle must answer:

1. Was verified cash collected?
2. What is the primary blocker?
3. Which safe action reduces that blocker the most?
4. Was that action executed and evidenced?

A cycle that cannot identify a primary blocker and a next best action is incomplete.

## Pivot rules

Pivot or stop a strategy when a bounded test produces no qualified signal, the expected net margin is unacceptable, the offer requires prohibited identity or legal commitments, or the channel cannot be used within current guardrails.

Accelerate only after a profitable verified payment or another explicitly approved strong commercial signal.

## Safety

No charcoal activity. No false identity, fake credentials, unauthorized account creation, KYC, contract, purchase, payment, legal commitment, destructive action, or out-of-scope cybersecurity activity. High-risk steps remain subject to explicit human validation.
