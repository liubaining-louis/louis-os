# Cash-first public source mesh

Louis OS must not depend on one marketplace or one work type. Public read-only adapters form a source mesh, and each adapter must independently preserve authoritative budget, deadline, competition and payment evidence.

## Current public lanes

- GitHub/Algora/Opire: canonical issue verification before ranking.
- Freelancer.com: explicit budget range, positive time remaining and bounded bids.
- Guru: explicit fixed or bounded hourly range, future quote deadline, bounded quotes and strong employer payment history.
- USA.gov challenges: strategic only unless scope and time-to-cash become genuinely cash-first.

## Routing rules

1. Reject stale, physical, sensitive, unsafe, vague, long-term, manual-only or unverified work.
2. Prefer work estimated at no more than 16 hours and payment within 30 days.
3. Prepare a complete proposal dossier before requesting any account or terms gate.
4. Ask only for the smallest exact human action after the dossier is ready.
5. Never count a proposal, award, contract, payout or revenue without a platform receipt.
6. Continue adding independent official sources; a source outage or empty result must not stop the rest of the mesh.
