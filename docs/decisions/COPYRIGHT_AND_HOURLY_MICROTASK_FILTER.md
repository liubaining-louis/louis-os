# Copyright and hourly microtask filter

## Decision

Louis OS rejects paid listings that require bulk copying, scraping, downloading, reproducing or republishing song lyrics, books, articles, paywalled content or similar protected works unless authoritative rights evidence is explicitly available.

It also rejects public hourly listings whose displayed minimum rate is below 10 USD, EUR or GBP per hour. This is a conservative prequalification floor for the cash-first lane, not a legal minimum-wage determination and not a client quote.

## Rationale

A production cycle surfaced a request to copy and clean 500 song lyrics from third-party lyrics websites for 4–8 USD per hour. The task was unsuitable because:

- mass reproduction of protected lyrics creates copyright risk;
- the work depended on third-party content rights that were not evidenced;
- the hourly compensation was below the project's cash-first economic floor;
- building a new capability for that task would not improve the probability of a lawful first payment.

## Truth rules

A rejection is recorded as a policy decision. It does not create a capability gap, proposal dossier, submission, pipeline value, conversion or revenue. Discovery continues on other sources.
