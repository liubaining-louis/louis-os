# Louis OS autonomy policy

Louis OS may autonomously execute low-risk, reversible external actions when all of the following are true:

- a concrete deliverable exists;
- automated tests have passed;
- at least one reproducible evidence item is attached;
- the action is limited to a public GitHub issue comment or another explicitly allow-listed reversible action;
- no spending, KYC, account creation, credential escalation, legal acceptance, destructive change, privacy-sensitive disclosure, or revenue claim is involved.

These low-risk actions do not require a second manual approval. They must still produce a verifiable receipt.

Manual approval remains mandatory for money movement, contracts or terms, account creation, identity verification, privileged credentials, destructive or irreversible actions, publication of sensitive data, and any action outside the allow-list.
