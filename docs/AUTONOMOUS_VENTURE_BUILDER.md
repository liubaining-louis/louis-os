# Autonomous Venture Builder (AVB)

## Permanent objective

Louis OS must evolve from producing recommendations into building and operating a remote venture through measurable, evidence-grounded cycles.

The system optimizes for the next authorized action that most increases the probability of validated revenue while minimizing capital, irreversible risk, and recurring human work.

## Cycle

1. **Observe** accessible evidence sources.
2. **Detect** concrete problems, demand signals, and constraints.
3. **Hypothesize** venture offers and acquisition mechanisms.
4. **Select** one hypothesis using explicit scoring and evidence coverage.
5. **Build** executable assets in the repository or approved connected systems.
6. **Experiment** through reversible actions; external actions require approval.
7. **Measure** observed outcomes only.
8. **Memorize** evidence, decisions, failures, and outcomes.
9. **Decide** keep, modify, pause, or abandon.
10. **Repeat** under budget and stop rules.

## Venture states

`observing → hypothesizing → selected → building → ready_for_test → approval_required → testing → measuring → learning → scaling | pivoted | stopped`

A transition must include its evidence references, decision reason, budget impact, and next measurable milestone.

## Safety and autonomy contract

- No invented contacts, market sizes, prices, revenue, conversion rates, or capabilities.
- External factual claims require evidence references.
- Sending, publishing, purchasing, contracting, and collecting payment require explicit approval until a dedicated policy permits them.
- Repeated work must target at least 80% automation and less than 30 minutes of human intervention per week.
- Every experiment has a budget, deadline, success threshold, stop condition, and idempotency key.
- Failed attempts are persisted to prevent blind repetition.
- Narrative output alone is not progress. A cycle must create or update an artifact, evidence record, experiment, metric, or decision state.

## Comparative benchmark

Issue #47 and its first completed answer are the frozen baseline. The same mission is rerun after each AVB milestone.

Evaluation dimensions:

- evidence grounding;
- unsupported-claim avoidance;
- specificity and coherent arithmetic;
- operational feasibility;
- autonomy realism;
- executable artifacts produced;
- measurable next action;
- explicit human dependency;
- complete observe-test-measure-learn loop.

No AVB version is promoted when its aggregate score is below the baseline or when unsupported claims increase.
