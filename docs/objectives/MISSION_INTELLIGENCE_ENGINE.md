# Mission Intelligence Engine — permanent objective

## North-star objective

Louis OS must maximize verified paid outcomes, not raw discovery volume.

Primary optimization target:

`expected verified revenue / (delivery hours + human actions + risk cost)`

The engine must learn from every opportunity lifecycle:

`observed -> qualified -> prepared -> submitted -> viewed -> replied -> negotiated -> won/lost -> delivered -> accepted -> paid`

No stage may be inferred without evidence. No simulated amount counts as pipeline or revenue.

## Required intelligence loops

1. **Outcome memory** — persist every lifecycle transition, timestamp, reason, receipt and verified monetary outcome.
2. **Expected-payment scoring** — estimate response, win and payment probabilities using evidence-backed features.
3. **Adaptive exploration/exploitation** — allocate search effort 70% to proven lanes, 20% to adjacent lanes and 10% to experiments, while preserving a minimum exploration budget.
4. **Product-led search** — search first for work matching validated reusable deliverables.
5. **Bounded proof generation** — prepare a small relevant proof without completing unpaid client work.
6. **Proposal experimentation** — record proposal variant and outcome; never fabricate engagement telemetry.
7. **Client-intent hypotheses** — separate observed facts from commercial hypotheses and confidence.
8. **Autonomy levels** — research and preparation are autonomous; identity, contractual acceptance, payment configuration and high-risk actions remain gated.
9. **Anti-stagnation control** — change source, offer, price, category or proposal strategy when activity does not produce economic progression.
10. **Economic truth dashboard** — prioritize submissions, replies, wins, accepted deliverables, verified payments, revenue per hour and human actions per payment.

## Promotion criteria

The engine is promoted only when deterministic tests pass and it:

- never converts simulations into pipeline or revenue;
- never submits stale or unverified opportunities;
- preserves append-only outcome history;
- explains every score through feature contributions;
- detects defined stagnation patterns;
- generates a bounded search allocation totaling 100%;
- preserves at least 10% exploration until sufficient paid evidence exists.

## Initial target

Within the first 30 verified submissions, learn which source, capability, offer, proposal style and client profile produce the highest probability of a lawful payment within 30 days.

The immediate operational milestone remains: obtain the first verified external submission receipt, then the first verified payment.
