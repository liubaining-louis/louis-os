# First Paid Mission Accelerator

## North-star objective

Obtain the first externally verified paid mission by minimizing time-to-contract, delivery risk, human actions and payment uncertainty.

## Internet-backed operating principles

1. Sell one narrow outcome, not a broad capability.
2. Prefer fixed-price, bounded work that can be demonstrated before purchase.
3. Use a work sample or a very small reversible proof, never a full unpaid delivery.
4. Apply only when identity, language, geography and account requirements are compatible.
5. Reject opportunities with unclear payment, stale status, excessive competition or duplicate active claims.
6. Prioritize channels where the hiring and payment path is explicit.
7. Treat the first mission as a trust-building transaction; optimize for a verifiable successful receipt before maximizing price.
8. Learn from every funnel transition: discovered, eligible, prepared, submitted, replied, hired, delivered, accepted, paid.

## Initial productized offers

### CSV Rescue — 49 to 149 EUR
- Deduplicate, normalize and validate one CSV or spreadsheet.
- Bounded by row count and explicit acceptance checks.
- Deliver cleaned file, validation report and reusable script when appropriate.

### API Connection Fix — 79 to 249 EUR
- Diagnose and fix one narrowly scoped API integration problem.
- Deliver patch, test evidence and rollback notes.

### Landing Page Repair — 79 to 199 EUR
- Fix one broken responsive page, form or deployment issue.
- Deliver before/after evidence and deployment instructions.

### Research Brief — 49 to 149 EUR
- Produce one source-backed market, supplier or competitor brief.
- Deliver concise report, source map and explicit uncertainty.

## Channel priority

1. Warm/direct prospects with an explicit small problem and payment mechanism.
2. Productized-service marketplaces and direct-contract platforms.
3. Newly posted small fixed-price marketplace jobs with verified client/payment signals.
4. Open-source bounties only when the issue is open, unclaimed, capability-compatible and has a precise merge/payment path.

## Hard gates

An opportunity cannot enter the action queue unless all are true:

- status is freshly verified open;
- payment mechanism is documented;
- deliverable and acceptance condition are clear;
- estimated effort is at most 8 hours for the first mission;
- Louis OS has a validated capability fit of at least 0.70;
- no unverifiable personal identity, language or geography requirement;
- no active competing claim when the channel is winner-takes-all;
- legal, ethical and platform-policy checks pass;
- the next human action, if any, is singular and explicit.

## Truth rule

Prepared work is not submitted work. Submitted work is not hired work. Hired work is not revenue. Revenue exists only after an external payment receipt is captured.
